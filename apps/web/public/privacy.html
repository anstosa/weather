Ballydídean Weather

Privacy policy

The weather is dramatic enough. Your privacy shouldn’t be.

Updated September 22, 2026

This covers the Ballydídean Weather website, app and widgets. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact Ballydídean Farm Sanctuary at sanctuary@ballydidean.farm.

Weather, not your whereabouts

Forecasts are for the farm, not your phone’s location. We don’t request GPS, contacts, camera or microphone access. No account is needed to check the weather.

What stays on your device

The website and app save display preferences and cached pages locally. Widgets store forecast data, refresh status and unit preferences. The native widget does not run Google Analytics; the website inside the app does.

Analytics and other services

Public production pages use Google Analytics 4 to understand usage. Google receives page activity, browser and device details, approximate location and cookie-based identifiers. We disable Google Signals and ad-personalization signals in our tag. This privacy page loads no analytics scripts. See how Google uses this information.

Our server and Cloudflare process IP addresses and request metadata to deliver and protect the service. We also use your IP to recognize the farm’s network for local-only displays. Maps request tiles directly from OpenStreetMap and USGS, which receive your IP and browser request information.

Security, retention and your choices

Connections use HTTPS. Administrator access is restricted, passwords are stored as hashes, and admin session cookies expire after 12 hours. No system is perfectly secure; ours does not control the weather, either.

Local preferences and cached pages remain until replaced or cleared. Widget forecasts are replaced on refresh; Android widget data is cleared when the last widget is removed. Clear browser site data or the app’s storage to remove local data. Blocking cookies or analytics requests limits tracking; Google also offers an Analytics browser opt-out.

Analytics and network-record retention depends on provider settings and policies, not one app-wide expiry. Contact us about access or deletion of information you’ve shared with us. We’ll update this page when our practices change.
